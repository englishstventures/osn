The invite builder's section nav drops its visibility dots. A section that
guests will not see — switched on but empty, or switched off — has its label
struck through instead, on the tabs and on the narrow-layout menu trigger, in
the same ink as a shown label, and each accessible name still says which
reason. A selected tab keeps its gold wash, and its ink moves from `text-gold`
to `text-gold-ink`.
